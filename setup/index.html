Set up Claude the way I use it

I use Claude to edit my vlogs, make thumbnails, write descriptions and even built my live views website. Follow these steps in order and you'll have the same setup, ready for your music.

About 30 minutesMac or Windows computerClaude Pro, $20 a month
0 of 8 done

  1. Make a Claude account and get Pro

    Go to claude.ai, sign up, then upgrade to Pro (the $20 a month plan).

    Pro is what lets Claude work on your own computer: your files, your music, your projects.

  2. Install the Claude desktop app

    Download it from claude.ai/download on your computer, open it and sign in with the same account.

    The desktop app is where all the real work happens. The phone app is just for talking to it.

  3. Make a folder for your music kit

    On your computer (or an external drive), make a new folder called music-kit. This is Claude's workspace: everything it builds for you lives here.

    Keep your songs and stems wherever they are now. Claude reads them in place.

  4. Open the Code tab and pick your folder

    In the desktop app, switch to the Code tab at the top, start a new session and choose your music-kit folder.

    This mode lets Claude run tools, make files and build things for you, instead of only chatting.

  5. Paste this first message

    Copy this into the session and send it. Claude will set itself up for you and ask you a few questions.

    I'm a music artist and I release my music through DistroKid. Set up this folder as my own kit, like my brother's vlog kit: a CLAUDE.md with my rules, skills for the things I do often, and a settings file. Install anything my computer needs and walk me through it step by step, one thing at a time, with plain words. Things I want help with: release planning, cover art ideas, captions and posts for my songs, short videos for TikTok and Reels, and a page that shows my total DistroKid earnings and streams.

  6. Answer its questions and let it install things

    Claude will ask about your music and may want to install free tools (like ffmpeg for audio and video). Say yes to the ones it explains.

    Two rules I follow: if a tool needs a password or an API key, you paste it into the file Claude tells you, never into the chat. And read what Claude wants to do before you approve it.

  7. Build your earnings page

    In DistroKid, go to Bank, then See excruciating detail, and download the TSV file. Then send Claude this:

    Build me a page that shows my total DistroKid earnings for my whole career, big like a digital clock on a wall, plus total streams, top songs and money by store. I'll drop in my DistroKid TSV file, and it should stay on my device, never uploaded. Make it look like a 3D music studio with me as a Lego-style character.

    DistroKid doesn't let apps read your earnings automatically, so you drop in a fresh file whenever you want it updated (about once a month).

  8. Control it from your phone

    In the desktop app, turn on Remote Control for your session. Then open the Claude app on your phone and you can keep talking to it while your computer does the work.

    Leave the computer on and the desktop app open while it works.
